Welcome ✈️

ThaiIn3Days is a beautifully crafted iOS app that teaches you the essential Thai phrases you need for a real trip to Thailand. Greetings, taxis, food, shopping, hotels, emergencies — all the everyday situations you will actually encounter, taught with native audio, illustrated dialogues and friendly cultural tips.
